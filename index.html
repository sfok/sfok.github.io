Hermes Personal Calendar

A privately operated, self-hosted assistant integration for the operator's personal calendar management. This is an informational website, not a public calendar service.

With the account holder's authorization, the integration can read, create, update and delete Google Calendar events, answer calendar questions and provide appointment reminders. Home Assistant can deliver spoken reminders at home.

The Google permission requested is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events. This permission covers events on calendars accessible to the authorized account; it is not restricted by Google to one personal calendar. It does not grant Gmail, Drive or Contacts access.

Calendar access requires explicit Google OAuth consent. No calendar data or credentials are published on this website.
