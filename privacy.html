Privacy policy — Hermes Personal Calendar

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Purpose and information accessed

This integration is operated for the account holder's personal use. Authorized calendar event information can include titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations and participant information returned by Google. It is used to answer calendar requests, manage appointments and calculate or deliver reminders.

Processing and service providers

The integration runs on the operator's own infrastructure and communicates with Google Calendar. Relevant request or event information may be processed by the assistant's configured AI provider when needed to answer a request. Home Assistant and the configured speech provider may process reminder text for spoken announcements. A routing service such as Waze may receive an origin and destination for travel-time estimates.

Calendar information is not sold, used for advertising or published on this site. This policy does not promise that all processing remains local.

Storage and access

OAuth credentials are stored in the operator's private Hermes environment, not in the website or its public source repository. Event information may remain in private assistant conversations, reminder state, logs or backups according to the operator's configuration. Those records require separate cleanup; revoking Google access does not automatically erase existing records.

Control and deletion

The account holder can revoke the application's access in Google Account security settings. Revocation prevents further authorized access but does not itself delete calendar events. The operator can separately remove locally retained conversations, reminder data and credentials.

This website and contact

This static site includes no application analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosting may process connection information under GitHub's own privacy policy.

Operator contact: sfok on GitHub. Do not send passwords, tokens or private appointment details through public GitHub interactions.
